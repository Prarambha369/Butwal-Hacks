# Product — Butwal Hacks

## Purpose
Butwal Hacks is a nonprofit technology initiative focused on decentralizing innovation, mentorship, and practical learning for youth in Butwal and Rupandehi, Nepal (Lumbini Province). The website serves as the public face of the organization.

## Mission & Vision
- Mission: Decentralize technology education and innovation in Nepal by providing hands-on learning and mentorship.
- Vision: Establish Butwal as a vibrant technical hub fostering innovation and social impact nationally.

## Key Features
- Landing page with hero, mission, events, and trusted-by sections
- Event pages with dedicated slugs and experience pages (e.g., Daydream Butwal September 2024)
- Blog with dynamic slug-based routing
- Initiatives listing and detail pages
- Community and member directory
- About, governance, and philosophy pages
- Donor recognition page
- Contact form with validation
- Docs / engineering documentation section
- Legal pages: privacy policy, terms of service, cookie policy
- Showcase and explore pages
- 77 Hacks event page
- Resources section
- Support page
- Light/dark theme toggle
- SEO metadata, sitemap, robots.txt
- Vercel Analytics + Google Analytics

## Target Users
- Youth in Butwal/Rupandehi interested in technology
- Volunteers and contributors to the initiative
- Donors and sponsors
- General public learning about the organization
