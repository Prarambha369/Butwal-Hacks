## What we are

Butwal Hacks is a youth-led, student-run technology initiative based in Butwal,
Rupandehi District, Lumbini Province, Nepal. We run free hackathons, workshops,
and project-based learning programmes for students.

- No participation fees, and no prior experience is required.
- Students lead: they organise the events, build the projects, and teach each
  other. It is not a staff-run programme for students.
- Community-funded. Every rupee is tracked publicly on Open Collective, and
  there are no administrative salaries — funds go to programmes.
- Open source. The platform's code is public on GitHub.

We are not a general-purpose social network, not a code hosting platform, and
not a course-based LMS. Learning here is project-based: every event ends with
something real that participants can show.

## Timeline

- **March 2025** — Prarambha Bashyal started the idea behind Butwal Hacks.
  Two co-founders, Hardik Chapagain and Rabin Chudali, joined the effort.
- **October 2025** — the team moved from planning into full in-field
  operations, running as a real organisation rather than an idea.
- **First event — Daydream Butwal.** Butwal Hacks' flagship hackathon, held in
  Butwal. This was the organisation's first event.
- **January 2026 — HackDay Gemini**, run in partnership with MLH (Major League
  Hacking), in Butwal. This was the second event.
- **Lumbini Province Police.** The Lumbini Province Police approached Butwal
  Hacks to help host a hackathon. Members of the Butwal Hacks team served as
  organisers of Nepal's first government-led hackathon.
