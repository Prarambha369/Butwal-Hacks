# Disciplinary Action Policy

The sanctions that can be applied when a policy is broken, how they are
notified, and how they can be appealed. Adapted from
[MLH's disciplinary action policy](https://github.com/MLH/mlh-policies/blob/main/disciplinary-action.md),
which is public and reusable with attribution.

Butwal Hacks reserves the right to refuse entry to an event, and to remove
anyone from an event or from the platform, to keep community spaces safe.

## The ladder

Actions are taken in order of severity, and in proportion to the impact of
the behaviour.

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning that explains what was wrong
   and why it was a problem. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with stated consequences for continued behaviour,
   and no interaction with the people involved — including the people
   enforcing the Code of Conduct — for a stated period. In an event
   context, this may include being asked to stay away from a specific
   person or area.
3. **End of event privileges.** Immediate removal of event responsibilities:
   no volunteering, no mentoring, no judging, no access to an organizer-only
   area.
4. **Temporary suspension.** Removal from the event, or from the platform,
   for a stated period.
5. **Permanent ban.** Removal from future Butwal Hacks events and from the
   platform, with no end date.

Repeat violations escalate. A permanent ban is the expected outcome for
safeguarding failures, and for attempts to conceal cheating.

## Notification

Anyone who receives a temporary suspension or a permanent ban is told in
writing, by email, with:

- The exact cause of the action.
- The length of the suspension. Bans are permanent.

## Registering a ban with an event

If someone who is suspended or banned tries to register for an event:

1. They are reminded, in writing, that they are not permitted to take part.
2. The event's lead organizer is notified, in writing, that the person is
   suspended or banned, and should not be admitted.
3. The organizers are given the facts they need to enforce it — the action and
   its dates — and nothing more.

In the interest of privacy, the **reason** for the action is not shared with
event organizers unless the person it was applied to asks for that to be
shared.

## Appeals

A temporary suspension or a permanent ban can be appealed by writing to
**legal@butwalhacks.com** with "[Appeal]" at the start of the subject
line, and attaching any evidence you want considered.

The appeal is heard by maintainers who were **not** involved in the original
decision. They may confirm the action, change it, or lift it. They will tell
you the outcome and the reason.

An appeal does not automatically pause the action. If you need the action
paused while the appeal is heard, say so in the appeal, and it will be
considered on its merits.

## Privacy

Disciplinary records are access-controlled. The identity of a reporter is
never disclosed. The identity of the person an action was applied to is
disclosed only to people who need it to enforce the action.
