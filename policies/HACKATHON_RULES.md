# Hackathon Rules

> The rules of the competition. Separate from the platform terms, on purpose. Effective 16 October 2026.

## What this is

The rules for taking part in a Butwal Hacks hackathon or event. These are separate from the [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md) because agreeing to use a website is not the same as agreeing to be judged in a competition.

**Where an event's own announcement conflicts with this document, the event announcement governs that event only.** We will say so clearly at the time.

## The spirit of the competition

A hackathon is a marathon. Some people come to win. Most come to get better and to enjoy themselves. Whichever you are here for, help beginners, share what you know, and treat the people around you well.

Hacking is about building. It is not about breaking into systems you do not own, taking credit for others' work, or putting a working community at risk for a prize.

## Eligibility

- The competition is open to individuals who are **at least 13 years old**.
- Anyone aged **13 to 17 must have a parent or guardian's consent** to participate. See [PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md](PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md).
- Participants are primarily **students**: anyone at a school, college, or university, in a bootcamp or similar programme, or who graduated within the last 12 months. Professionals and recent graduates are welcome.
- Each participant may be on **one team only**.
- A team normally has **two to four active members**, and every member must actively contribute.
- Organizers, volunteers, judges, and sponsors **may not compete**. This applies to anyone in a privileged position at the event.
- Sponsors and partner organisations may not enter teams.

## Eligibility with an age threshold

Where an event sets an age threshold for eligibility of work or awards, work, submissions, or progress made **on or after** that birthday does not count toward the event and is not eligible for an award. Events state the threshold in their announcement.

## The rules

1. **Everyone agrees to uphold the** [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and, where they are under 18, the [Safeguarding Policy](SAFEGUARDING_POLICY.md).
2. **Work on your project must be done during the event period.** Ideas you had beforehand are welcome and encouraged.
3. **Use open-source libraries, frameworks, and previously available code freely.** Pre-opening a project solely to use its code during the event is against the spirit and is not allowed.
4. **Do not re-use code or project material from a previous project of your own** in a way that misrepresents the work as new.
5. **Building something that already exists is allowed.** Hacks do not have to be original. If someone wants to build a common idea, they should be judged on quality, not novelty.
6. **Stop hacking when time is up.** Debugging and small fixes are permitted afterwards. Adding features or making substantial changes is not.
7. **Use a pre-existing idea.** You may.
8. **Your code must be publicly available**, ideally in a public git repository.
9. **Your code must stay public after the event** to be eligible for prizes. If a repository or demo video is not public, a new winner may be selected.
10. **Teams may be disqualified** at the organizers' discretion, including for breaking these rules, breaking the Code of Conduct, or unsporting behaviour.
11. **Do not attack the infrastructure.** Do not stress-test, scan, or attack Butwal Hacks, event, sponsor, or venue systems, or anyone else's, without written authorisation.
12. **Do not use another person's data, credentials, or identity.**

## AI tools and generated code

Permitted, with three conditions:

- You are responsible for the code you submit and must be able to explain it.
- Do not submit another person's work as your own.
- Do not use a generative model to produce content that misrepresents who did the work.

Submitting AI-generated work you cannot explain risks disqualification.

## Submission

- Submit through the platform before the deadline. Late, lost, or incomplete submissions risk disqualification at the organizers' discretion.
- A submission typically includes a description, a link to the repository, and a demo video.
- **Include screenshots or a demo.** A project nobody can see is a project that cannot be judged.
- You must have the right to submit everything you submit, and you must be able to publish it publicly.

## Judging

Projects are judged by an organizer or judge against criteria announced at the event. Typical criteria are technical achievement, usefulness, design, and the quality of the pitch.

Judges' decisions are final on matters relating to the competition. If the event does not publish a specific rubric, judges weigh each project holistically.

## Prizes and bounties

- Prizes and bounties are **not guaranteed** and depend on available funding. Sponsors fund bounties separately from organizer prizes.
- A prize may be withdrawn where a team is disqualified.
- **AI-assisted work cannot win a prize judged on skill** unless the event announcement says otherwise.
- Payment to a **minor** requires a verified guardian-provided payout account. See [PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md](PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md).
- Tax and foreign-exchange consequences are the winner's responsibility.
- A prize does not imply endorsement of a project by Butwal Hacks or a sponsor.

## Publicity

By submitting, you agree your submission may be published to announce winners and promote Butwal Hacks. You keep all rights in your work; see the intellectual property section of the [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). You may withdraw promotional use by emailing **legal@butwalhacks.com**.

## Rules may be changed

Organizers may change these rules for an event, and may disqualify a participant or team whose conduct they judge, in their sole discretion, contrary to the spirit of these rules or the Code of Conduct, including deducting progress markers, refusing an award, or removing the team from the event.

We will announce changes at the event. Changes never apply retroactively to work already judged.

## Moderation

We may, at our sole discretion and without limitation, disqualify any application or submission we deem inappropriate, objectionable, or inconsistent with the community we are trying to build.
