# Data Subject Rights

> How to see, correct, delete, or restrict your data, and what we must do about it. Effective 16 October 2026.

## What this is

The procedure for exercising your rights under the **Law on Protection of Personal Data 2025** and the **Privacy Act 2075 (2018)**, with the deadlines the law sets us.

## Quick actions

**Email privacy@butwalhacks.com**, stating your username and what you want. One email is enough. You do not need to use a form or write to a particular person.

| Your request | What we must do | Deadline |
|--------------|-----------------|----------|
| Acknowledge your request | Confirm receipt to you | **2 working days** |
| Tell you what we hold, and for what purpose | Provide a copy, with categories, purposes, and recipients | **2 working days** |
| Correct inaccurate data | Fix it | **15 days** |
| Delete your data | Stop processing and delete it | **15 days** |
| Cease processing where you withdraw consent | Stop, and tell you how | **15 days** |
| Restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests | Stop, or explain why we cannot | **15 days** |
| Restrict processing while a dispute is investigated | Pause the disputed processing | **15 days** |
| Nominate someone to act for you | Confirm the arrangement with them | **15 days** |

Two working days is the period for responding with information or acknowledgement. Fifteen days is the period for actually implementing a change. Where the law allows an extension for complexity, we will tell you why and give you a revised date.

## How to make a request

Include whichever of these you have. If you cannot find the last one, tell us your username and we will find you.

- **Your account username or the `slug_id` on your public profile** — this is the most reliable
- The email address associated with your account
- What you want done
- The email address or postal address to send the reply to

### Verifying who you are

We must confirm you are the person whose data you are asking about before we can act, because responding to the wrong person would itself be a breach.

We accept verification by:

- replying from the email address on the account, **or**
- signing in to the account, **or**
- if neither is possible, telling us two details already on your profile that we can check

We will never ask for your password, your Auth0 credentials, or a full payment card number, and no member of Butwal Hacks will ask you for them.

If verification is not possible, we will explain what else would satisfy us. We may ask for a signed declaration or an alternative proof of identity.

### If you are under 18

You may make any request in this document **yourself**, without a parent or guardian. We will not require a guardian to speak on your behalf, and we will not ask a guardian for permission before helping you.

A parent or guardian may also make a request on your behalf. See [PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md](PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md).

### If someone acts for you

You may nominate another person to exercise your rights. We will confirm the arrangement with you and with them before acting, and will share only the data necessary to establish it. The nomination can be withdrawn at any time.

## Your rights in detail

### Be informed

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, why we hold it, what legal basis we rely on, how long we keep it, who we have shared it with, whether we have transferred it outside Nepal and on what safeguard, and whether we use automated decision-making.

### View and obtain a copy

We will provide your personal data in a readable, portable form. Where we process it because you consented, we will provide the consent record: when it was given, how it was captured, and how it was verified.

### Correct

Ask us to correct anything inaccurate, incomplete, or out of date. Where a credential or marker was issued wrongly, say so and we will correct or revoke it.

### Delete

Ask us to delete your data. We will delete:

- your account and profile data
- your unpublished content
- event registrations, except attendance records we must keep for a dispute or legal claim
- session and rate-limit data on its normal cycle

Where we must retain something — an audit record of a moderation action, a record tied to a legal claim, a financial record where the law requires it — we will tell you exactly what we kept, why, and for how long, and we will restrict it to storage only.

### Withdraw consent and cease processing

You can withdraw consent at any time, for any processing that relies on it. This includes sensitive data, optional public profile fields, AI features, and any consent given by a guardian for a person under 18.

We will stop the processing and delete the resulting data within fifteen days. We will tell you how to do it for each feature.

### Restrict and object

You can ask us to pause processing while we investigate a dispute, or to object to processing we base on legitimate interests. We will stop, or explain in writing why we cannot and what alternative we can offer.

### Nominate someone

Appoint another person to make requests on your behalf, for example a parent, guardian, or carer.

### Complain

You can complain to us, and you can complain to the authority designated under the Law on Protection of Personal Data, or to the competent police or judicial authorities where you believe an offence has been committed. Complaining to us does not stop you complaining to an authority, and doing so is not held against you.

We aim to acknowledge a complaint within 30 days and resolve it within 60 days. If we need longer we will tell you why.

## What happens when you ask us to delete your account

1. We confirm receipt within **two working days**.
2. We tell you what will be deleted and what, if anything, we must retain.
3. We delete within **fifteen days** and confirm in writing.
4. Your public profile, projects, and credentials stop appearing publicly.
5. Aggregated, non-identifiable statistics are not affected, because they cannot be traced back to you.

If your account is linked to a team or project with other members, deleting your account does not delete work you contributed to a shared project. Ask us if you want your contributions removed from someone else's project, and we will take that up with them.

## Deleting a child's data

A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a minor's account and data at any time. The same fifteen-day deadline applies. If you believe we hold a minor's data without a guardian's consent, tell us and we will treat it as urgent and investigate.

## Our commitments to you

- **We do not charge** for a first request, and we will not charge for exercising a statutory right.
- **We do not require a particular format.**
- **We do not make you repeat the same request** for the same thing.
- **We do not use your request as a reason to reduce your service.** Withdrawing consent to a feature simply means you do not get that feature.
- **We will not retaliate** against you for exercising a right or complaining.

## Complaints about how we handle a request

If you are unhappy with our response, reply to the same email asking for review by a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision. If you remain unhappy, you may complain to the relevant authority. Contact details for the designated authority are pending confirmation and are tracked in [COMPLIANCE_GAPS.md](COMPLIANCE_GAPS.md).
