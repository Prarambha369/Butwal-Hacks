# Safeguarding Policy

> Our commitments to young participants, and the boundaries maintainers, organizers, and mentors must keep. Effective 16 October 2026.

## What this is

The rules that protect children and at-risk young people taking part in Butwal Hacks. It applies to every event, online or in person, and to everyone acting for Butwal Hacks: maintainers, organizers, volunteers, mentors, judges, speakers, and venue staff.

This is not decoration. Most people in this community are students, and some are minors. A clear safeguarding policy is how we make the community safe for them.

## Our core commitments

1. **The safety and welfare of minors is the paramount consideration.** It overrides an event schedule, a sponsor request, or a maintainer's convenience.
2. **No one is alone with a minor.** Physical, digital, and conversational boundaries apply equally.
3. **All communication with participants goes through official channels** — our platform, our registered email addresses, or event Slack/Discord workspaces. Never a personal phone, personal social account, or a messaging app.
4. **Parents and guardians are informed** about this policy, our emergency procedures, and how to reach us, before the event.
5. **Consent is obtained and recorded before an event, not assumed on the day.**

## Who this covers

Everyone present at any Butwal Hacks activity:

- participants aged 13 to 17, and adults who attended as minors
- parents and guardians of participants
- organizers, event staff, and volunteers
- mentors, guest speakers, judges, and sponsors
- external vendors and venue staff working on our behalf

This applies to gender identity, sex, ethnicity, disability, race, caste, colour, language, and any other characteristic, and to every location where our activity happens, including online.

## Before an event

### Required for every minor participant

A guardian must have completed and signed the consent form described in [PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md](PARTICIPANT_AND_GUARDIAN_TERMS.md), providing:

- participant's name and age
- an emergency contact reachable **during the event**
- necessary medical or support needs, including allergies and medication
- any restriction on the participant leaving the venue
- media consent, as a separate optional permission

**Arrival and departure must be logged** — time, participant name, guardian contact, and that consent is on file. Arrival and departure logs are held for the event and destroyed within 30 days.

### Required for organizers

Before an event begins, an organizer must:

- brief every volunteer and mentor on this policy, and confirm they have read it
- confirm at least two staff are present at all times
- identify the safeguarding lead for the event and tell participants who it is
- post emergency contacts and the venue address where participants can see them
- agree the protocol for a participant leaving the venue mid-event
- confirm how medical or support needs will be met, and who holds that information

## Staff and mentor boundaries

These are absolute.

- **Never be alone with a minor.** Not in a venue, not in a vehicle, not in a call, not in a direct message.
- **Stay in view of other staff** whenever you are with a minor.
- **Never share personal contact details** with a participant — no personal phone number, no personal social account, no personal messaging handle.
- **Do not meet participants privately** outside a staffed event space.
- **Do not transport a minor alone.** Where transport is necessary, two adults travel with them.
- **Do not give gifts, money, or financial advantage** to a participant or their family. Anything of value must be handled through the organizers and recorded.
- **Physical contact is not permitted** except where necessary for immediate first aid.
- **Do not use your position** for personal, romantic, or social contact with a participant.
- **Do not photograph a minor individually** unless you have specific media consent.

These apply equally to women and men, to mentors and to organizers, and to in-person and online settings.

## Communication

- All official communication with participants is through our platform, our `@butwalhacks.com` addresses, or the event workspace.
- A participant may always ask who they are speaking to and what their role is.
- An organizer must answer a direct message from a participant within 24 hours, or pass it to the safeguarding lead.
- If a participant reports a concern, **believe them, do not investigate, and escalate immediately**.

## Leaving an event

A minor may leave an event at any time and must never be prevented from doing so.

- **A minor may not be restrained, or asked to wait, to resolve a disagreement with an adult.** If they want to leave, they leave. An organizer may help them contact a guardian.
- The exit protocol must be agreed in advance by organizers and communicated to guardians in advance.
- Where a participant under 16 leaves without their guardian, we contact the guardian and offer the participant a staffed, safe place to wait while we reach them. **They may leave at any time, and staff must not delay or prevent their departure** — the offer is there for their comfort, not to keep them there.
- Where a participant of any age leaves distressed, the safeguarding lead is informed immediately.

## Concerns, disclosures, and escalation

### If a participant raises a concern

1. **Listen, and do not promise secrecy.**
2. **Believe them.** Do not ask them to prove it, and do not investigate.
3. **Do not confront** the person they have named.
4. **Escalate the same day** to the event safeguarding lead, or to **conduct@butwalhacks.com** if no lead is present.

### If an adult reports a concern about another adult

1. Record what was said, in the reporter's own words, on the day.
2. Escalate the same day to **conduct@butwalhacks.com**.
3. Do not discuss the allegation with the person named beyond what the escalation requires.

### Our response

- We will acknowledge a safeguarding report **within 24 hours**.
- We will preserve relevant records and suspend account or event access **pending investigation**.
- We will not disclose the reporter's identity beyond what is necessary to conduct the investigation or meet a legal duty.
- We will support the participant in choosing the outcome, and will not require them to continue attending an event if they do not want to.
- Where a child may be at risk of harm, we will follow Nepali law and may contact the police or child protection authorities. **We will tell the parent or guardian unless doing so would risk the child.**

### Confidentiality

Every person who receives a safeguarding report is bound to confidentiality. A report is never shared with the wider team beyond those who need to know.

## Photography and media

- Media consent is **separate** from consent to participate and may be refused without affecting participation.
- Photography is of the event, not of individuals. Avoid close-ups of individual minors.
- Photography in restrooms, changing areas, or medical situations is prohibited.
- Photos must not be used in marketing without the specific media consent that covers it.

## Screening

Anyone working directly with minors must be:

- aged 18 or over
- subject to a proportionate criminal record and safeguarding background check, where such a check is available and lawful in Nepal

**What counts as supervised.** Working with a minor while another responsible adult is present, and able to see and hear the interaction, counts as supervised. Working with a minor alone does not, whatever the setting.

**Which roles this covers.** Every role that works directly with minors: mentors, volunteers, judges running one-to-one sessions, and sponsor staff with participant contact. It does not cover roles with no direct participant contact, such as sponsor marketing staff.

**Where we cannot run the check ourselves.** We do not operate background checking. Where a check is available to us, we ask for confirmation that it has been done before the person works with minors; where it is not, we use reference checks and supervision instead, and we tell the event team which of those applies.

**Prohibited:** any person convicted of a child-related offence, or subject to a current safeguarding ban, from working with minors at Butwal Hacks, whether paid or unpaid.

## Training

- Maintainers and organizers must read this policy and the Code of Conduct before taking on a role.
- Organizers must brief their team before each event.
- Training records are kept by the maintainers.

## Breaching this policy

A breach is handled under the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) enforcement process, with these additional consequences available:

- immediate removal from the event without notice
- suspension or permanent removal from the Butwal Hacks team
- permanent ban from Butwal Hacks spaces
- removal of an issued credential, trust marker, or certificate
- referral to police or child protection authorities where the law requires

For the avoidance of doubt: **a safeguarding breach may result in immediate removal and does not require the graduated process that an ordinary Code of Conduct matter receives.**

## Reporting a concern

| Purpose | Contact |
|---------|---------|
| Safeguarding concern, any time | conduct@butwalhacks.com |
| Urgent, and someone is at immediate risk | Nepal Police: **100**, or 112 |
| Legal notice | legal@butwalhacks.com |

You will not be penalised for making a report in good faith, even if it turns out to be unfounded.
