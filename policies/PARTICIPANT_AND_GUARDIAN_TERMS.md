# Participant and Guardian Terms

> Consent, rights, and responsibilities for members under 18 and the adults who consent for them. Effective 16 October 2026.

## What this is

The terms that apply when a member of Butwal Hacks is **under 18 years of age**. In Nepali law, a person under 18 is a minor, and a minor cannot enter into an agreement on their own. A parent or legal guardian must agree on their behalf.

This document exists because most of this community is students. We would rather be explicit about a minor's rights than discover later that we had assumed they were absent.

## The legal position

Under the **Privacy Act 2075 (2018)**, the consent of a guardian or curator is required before a minor's personal data is processed. We also hold ourselves to the rules that matter most here: consent must be verifiable and recorded, may not be obtained through default or misleading settings, and must be as easy to withdraw as it was to give. (Nepal has no comprehensive personal data protection statute in force that we are aware of, so these are our own commitments — see [COMPLIANCE_GAPS.md](COMPLIANCE_GAPS.md).)

We take two things from this. First, a guardian's agreement is not a formality. Second, we must be able to show we obtained it and can undo it.

## ⚠️ What is not yet enforced in the product

The Platform **does not currently ask for age at signup, and does not record a guardian relationship.** We cannot automatically distinguish a minor's account from an adult's, and we cannot enforce a consent gate at registration.

We are not going to describe an unenforced control as if it were enforced. This gap is tracked as priority 1 in [COMPLIANCE_GAPS.md](COMPLIANCE_GAPS.md), and the plan to close it is set out below.

Until it is closed:

1. **A guardian must complete the consent below before a minor creates an account.**
2. **A guardian is responsible for supervising the minor's use of the Platform.**
3. **A guardian may ask us to close the account and delete the data at any time**, and we will do so within fifteen days.

## Consent by a parent or guardian

By signing the consent at the end of this document, a parent or legal guardian of a minor agrees to the following on the minor's behalf.

### What you are consenting to

The guardian permits the minor named below to:

- create and use a Butwal Hacks account
- publish a profile, projects, skills, and credentials under their control
- register for and attend Butwal Hacks events
- join teams and take part in hackathons
- upload photographs, project media, and certificates
- use the AI assistant, team matching, and certificate text extraction
- receive operational emails about events they have registered for

### What we will do

We will:

- collect the minor's data only for those purposes
- publish only what the minor or you choose to make public
- never sell or trade the minor's data, and never use it for advertising
- never knowingly permit behavioural advertising to the minor
- keep the minor's audit and safety records for no longer than stated in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md)
- restrict maintainer access to the minor's data to what the role requires
- honour a withdrawal of consent

### What we will not do

We will not:

- publish the minor's full legal name on a public profile as a default
- share the minor's data with sponsors or advertisers
- use the minor's photograph in marketing without separate, specific consent
- allow a maintainer or mentor to contact the minor privately outside official channels
- require the minor to disclose caste, ethnicity, religion, health, or any government identity number

### Education data

Where the minor's school, college, or university is recorded, you acknowledge that education data is treated with care under Nepali law. The minor controls whether their school is displayed publicly, and you may ask us to remove it.

## In-person events: separate, signed consent

For **any in-person event**, online consent is not sufficient. A guardian must complete and sign a separate consent form before the event, providing:

- the participant's name and age
- an emergency contact reachable during the event
- any necessary medical or support needs, including allergies and medication
- any restriction on the participant leaving the venue
- consent to photography and media release, as a distinct, optional permission

Emergency contact and medical information are held for the duration of the event and destroyed within 30 days of it ending. We do not keep them afterwards. See [SAFEGUARDING_POLICY.md](SAFEGUARDING_POLICY.md).

## Photographs and media

Media consent is **separate** from consent to participate, and a guardian may refuse it without affecting the minor's participation.

If you consent to media, you agree that Butwal Hacks may photograph and record the minor at events and publish those images on our site and channels. You may withdraw this consent at any time by emailing **legal@butwalhacks.com**; we will stop new use, but cannot retract material already published or shared.

If you do not consent to media, the minor may still participate fully. They will be identified in event photography only incidentally, and we will not deliberately feature them.

## The minor's own rights

A minor's rights are real and are not replaced by a guardian's consent.

- **The minor may withdraw consent themselves.** We will accept this and will not require the guardian to agree.
- **The minor may ask us to delete their account and data**, and we will do so within fifteen days.
- **The minor may ask any question about their data** and receive a plain-language answer.
- **The minor may complain** about how their data is handled, to us or to a Nepali authority, without a guardian speaking on their behalf.
- **The minor's right to safety overrides an adult's convenience.** A minor may always leave an event and must never be prevented from doing so.

Where a minor's wishes conflict with a guardian's, we will listen to the minor, and where the law requires a guardian's decision we will follow the law.

## Prizes, bounties, and payments

- Prize eligibility is governed by the [Hackathon Rules](HACKATHON_RULES.md).
- Where a minor wins a monetary prize or bounty, payment requires a verified guardian-provided payout account, and the guardian enters into that arrangement.
- **We do not take payment details from a minor.** A guardian must provide them.
- Tax and foreign-exchange consequences of receiving a payment are for the guardian to consider with an adviser.

## Age changes

If a participant turns 18 during an event, participation continues on these terms until the end of that event. Where an event's rules set an age threshold for eligibility of work or awards, that threshold governs, and work done after reaching it may not count. See [HACKATHON_RULES.md](HACKATHON_RULES.md).

## Withdrawal and deletion

A guardian may withdraw consent and request deletion at any time:

1. Email **privacy@butwalhacks.com** from the address associated with the account, or **legal@butwalhacks.com** from any address, stating the minor's username.
2. **We verify the request before we act on it.** An email from any address, plus a username, is not enough to delete a minor's account: we first confirm the requester's identity and their authority over the minor, using the checks in [DATA_SUBJECT_RIGHTS.md](DATA_SUBJECT_RIGHTS.md). We will tell you which checks we need, and we will not delete on request alone. Where a guardian's consent is on file, we will act on it without a further check.
3. We will acknowledge within two working days.
4. We will cease processing and delete the data within fifteen days, except records we must keep for legal, security, or dispute purposes, which we will restrict rather than use.
5. We will confirm completion in writing.

If a guardian believes a minor's data is being processed without consent, contact **legal@butwalhacks.com**. We will treat that as urgent.
